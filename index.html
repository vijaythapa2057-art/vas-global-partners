Our Services

Everything your US or Australian business needs — bookkeeping, payroll, AP/AR, BAS preparation and tax filing, at a cost far below hiring locally.

Full Bookkeeping

Daily transaction processing, bank reconciliations, GL maintenance, month-end close and financial statements — on Xero, QuickBooks or MYOB.

Payroll Processing

Accurate, timely payroll for US and Australian teams — including STP (Single Touch Payroll), superannuation, PAYG withholding and payslip generation.

AP & AR Management

Vendor invoice processing and payments (AP), customer invoicing and collections follow-up (AR) to keep your cash flow healthy.

BAS Preparation (Australia only)

Accurate Business Activity Statement (BAS) preparation and lodgement support — GST reconciliation, PAYG instalments and fuel tax credits included.

Tax Filing Preparation

Workpaper preparation, income tax schedules and compliance support for US 1040/1120/1065 returns and Australian company/individual tax returns — ready for your CPA/CA to review and lodge.

Financial Reporting

Monthly P&L, balance sheet, cash flow statements, and custom KPI dashboards to give you a clear picture of your business health.
