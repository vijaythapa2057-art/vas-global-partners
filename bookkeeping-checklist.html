❓ Questions to Ask Before Outsourcing Your Bookkeeping

  1. Do they have experience with businesses in your country (US/Australia) and your industry?
  2. Are they familiar with your accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks, MYOB)?
  3. Will you have a dedicated point of contact, or will your work go to a shared pool?
  4. What's their turnaround time for monthly close and BAS preparation?
  5. Do they sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before receiving your financial data?
  6. How do they handle errors? What is their quality review process?
  7. Are their fees transparent and fixed, or do they charge by the hour?
  8. Can they provide client references from businesses similar to yours?

🚩 Red Flags in a Bookkeeper's Work

  • ✗ Unexplained entries categorised as "miscellaneous" or "other"
  • ✗ Bank account not reconciled each month
  • ✗ Accounts receivable ageing report shows invoices past 90+ days with no action
  • ✗ BAS lodged late or incorrect GST coding
  • ✗ Payroll liabilities appear on balance sheet with no explanation
  • ✗ Retained earnings fluctuate wildly with no supporting journal entries
  • ✗ No documentation or workpapers to support transactions
  • ✗ Slow response times and vague answers to straightforward questions
